# F04-C01 — Preserve concurrent correction recovery

State: verified

## Outcome

Rejected concurrent corrections retain a reachable conflict form and input instead of redirecting to 404.

## Prerequisites

Independent F04-T01/T02 working-tree review on 16 October 2026.

## Boundaries

Pay, benchmark and operational correction form recovery only. Existing authorisation, append-only history and stale-write protection continue.

## Contracts

A permitted correction URL remains readable after its original entry is superseded. It shows a conflict warning and retained input; submission is unavailable until the editor explicitly reloads current history. Other-person or missing correction IDs remain inaccessible. Actions still reject stale/superseded references.

## Acceptance criteria

Two editors can open the same correction; after one succeeds, the other is rejected and follows the redirect to a visible conflict, with original input and explicit discard/reload. No second correction commits.
