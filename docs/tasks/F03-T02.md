## Acceptance criteria

- Approved worked examples match; missing/N/A/real zero differ and required omissions prevent final submission.
- Changing salary/market/operational data cannot change the skills score; excluded/unassessed inputs cannot silently become zero.
- Reproducible generated tests prove bounds, order independence, determinism and monotonicity; failing seeds shrink and become regressions.
